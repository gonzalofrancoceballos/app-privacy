Privacy policy

13 November 2026

This app keeps your tasks, notes, projects and documents on your phone and in your own iCloud.

We run no server for this app, and we don't collect, see or sell anything you put in it.

This app has no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Backups you make go to your own iCloud Drive, in the app's own folder.

If you connect an agent, it reads and adds to your space from the computer where you run it. What happens to your data there is between you and that agent's provider.

While you test through TestFlight, Apple sends us crash reports and any feedback you choose to send. We use them only to fix faults.

Questions: open an issue at github.com/gonzalofrancoceballos/app-privacy.
